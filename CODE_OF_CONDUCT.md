# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in MEGA DataGate a respectful experience for everyone.

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include being considerate, giving constructive feedback, acknowledging different experience levels, and focusing criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, personal attacks, doxxing, discriminatory language, and publishing another person's private data.

Maintainers may remove or reject contributions that violate these standards. For concerns, contact the repository maintainers through the private reporting channel listed in the repository profile.
